When AI starts to matter

The challenge is rarely a lack of ideas. It is knowing what deserves to become real.

Scale-ups usually reach AI with experiments already happening across the business. The difficult part is deciding what should become part of the company, what should stay simple, and what is worth doing properly.

01

Too many experiments

Teams are already trying AI, but the useful ideas are mixed in with one off tools and disconnected pilots.

02

Systems are getting more complex

More customers, more data and more teams create more places where information and decisions need to connect.

03

Internal capacity is limited

You may not need a full AI team yet, but the systems still need serious technical thinking if they are going to last.
